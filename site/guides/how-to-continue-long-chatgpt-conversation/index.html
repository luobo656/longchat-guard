How do you continue a long ChatGPT conversation in a new chat?

The cleanest handoff is usually not a full transcript. A better continuation contains the current working state: what has been decided, what still matters, what remains unresolved, and what should happen next.

Short answer: summarize the current state, preserve constraints and decisions, reference the important source material, and start the new conversation with one concrete next task. LongChat Guard includes a continuation-prompt workflow to make this handoff easier.

A compact long-chat handoff structure

Goal

What the overall project or conversation is trying to accomplish.

Decisions and constraints

What has already been decided, what must remain true, and what should not be changed casually.

Current state and next action

What has been completed, what remains open, and exactly what the new conversation should do next.

What not to carry forward

Repeated discussion

Long debates that ended in a clear decision can usually be reduced to the decision and its rationale.

Obsolete attempts

Failed paths only need to be retained when they are important to avoid repeating the same mistake.

Unnecessary raw history

Copying the whole transcript can recreate the same long-context problem you are trying to solve.

This workflow is about conversation organization. LongChat Guard does not claim that moving to a new chat is required at any exact official OpenAI token or context threshold.
