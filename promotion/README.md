## Positioning

LongChat Guard is **not** a chat search, bookmark, export, or page-trimming extension. It solves a narrower question:

> Is this long ChatGPT working thread still comfortable to continue, or is it time to organize the working state and hand off to a fresh conversation?

The current product compares browser-local conversation load with a user-confirmed historical length-limit reference and learns reliable whole-turn growth. It shows simple risk states rather than pretending to know an official OpenAI quota.

## Launch rules

1. Do not promise an official token limit, exact context window, exact remaining quota, or guaranteed failure point.
2. Do not call LongChat Guard an OpenAI or ChatGPT official product.
3. Never translate the canonical brand “LongChat Guard”.
4. Do not paste the same promotional post into every community. Use the channel-specific drafts in this directory and participate in the discussion.
5. Lead with the problem and methodology, not “install my extension”.
6. Use real product screenshots only.
7. When discussing privacy, say that visible conversation content is processed locally after affirmative consent and raw chat text is not persisted or uploaded to a LongChat Guard analysis server.
8. Link to the methodology page when someone asks how the risk signal works.

## Success metrics

Traffic is not the only signal. Track:
- indexed pages;
- non-brand impressions;
- clicks and CTR;
- positions for long-conversation/context-window/problem queries;
- Chrome/Edge listing views and installs;
- uninstall rate;
- GitHub stars/issues;
- independent mentions and backlinks;
- whether AI/search systems accurately describe the product and its limitations.
